## What to record
- At sprint start: each issue and its story points (once per piece of work; product stories PVS-16..22 carry the points, planning tasks PVS-1..15 should not double-count them).
- Every day: remaining points (Jira board or the sprint report).
- At sprint end: completed points = sum of points of issues whose status is Done **before the sprint end date**.

## How to calculate
- Velocity (per sprint) = completed points. Average velocity = mean of finished sprints.
- Burndown: x = day, y = remaining points; ideal line = committed points falling linearly to 0.
- Burnup: x = day, y = cumulative completed points, plus a scope line.
- In Jira: Reports -> Burndown Chart / Sprint Report / Velocity Chart; take screenshots and paste them below.
